Automatic Chicken Farm Lighting Control System

The Automatic Chicken Farm Lighting Control System simulates natural sunrise and sunset patterns to reduce stress and improve flock welfare. Featuring customizable, flicker-free LED schedules, this system provides the exact lighting needed to stimulate egg production in layer chickens and ensure steady, healthy growth in broiler chickens.

Dimming
0–100%
Programs

Highlights & Features

  • Simulated Natural Lighting. The dawn-to-dusk dimming simulation eliminates sudden light shocks, minimizing panic, stress, and pecking behaviors among the chickens.
  • Increased Production Yield. Scientifically optimized lighting programs stimulate the reproductive system of layer chickens and help regulate the feeding cycles of broiler chickens.
  • Energy-Efficient LED Technology. Utilizes specialized, long-lasting poultry LED bulbs that consume less electricity while providing the exact light spectrum chickens need.
  • Fully Programmable Control. The smart control panel allows farm managers to set custom daily lighting schedules tailored to the specific growth stage of the chickens.
  • Harsh Environment Ready. All lighting fixtures are highly waterproof and dustproof, engineered to withstand the humid and ammonia-rich environment of a chicken house.

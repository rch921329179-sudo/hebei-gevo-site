MANUAL MANURE REMOVAL LAYER CAGE · H-TYPE

H Type Manual Manure Removal Chicken Layer Cage

The H-Type Manual Manure Removal Layer Cage is an innovative housing solution designed for medium-scale poultry farms aiming to maximize stocking density without the high capital investment required for fully automated systems. Utilizing a vertical H-frame structure, this system significantly increases land utilization compared to traditional A-frame cages. By replacing expensive automated conveyor belts with specialized manure dropping boards between tiers, it allows for efficient manual cleaning. This system offers a perfect balance between high-capacity egg production, space optimization, and budget control.

Highlights & Features

  • High Density & Space Saving. The vertical H-frame design allows for a higher number of birds per square meter, maximizing farm capacity.
  • Cost-Effective Investment. Eliminates the cost of motorized manure removal machinery, significantly lowering the initial setup expenditure.
  • Labor Flexibility. Ideal for regions with accessible agricultural labor, reducing reliance on electricity and mechanical maintenance.
  • Optimal Farm Hygiene. Equipped with smooth, durable dropping boards that make manual scraping quick and efficient, helping to maintain low ammonia levels and a healthy environment.
  • Strict Zone Separation. Feed is placed securely in dedicated troughs, while eggs roll out onto completely separate bottom meshes, preventing contamination and egg damage.

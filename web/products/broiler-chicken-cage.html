Broiler Chicken Cage

The Specialized Broiler Cage System is designed for highly efficient meat chicken production, accommodating birds from the day-old stage right through to harvest weight. The system utilizes an optimized feeding and ventilation structure that promotes rapid weight gain and flock uniformity. The flexible bottom mesh design ensures bird comfort, minimizes breast blisters, and allows manure to drop through easily, maintaining a clean and hygienic growing environment.

Density

Highlights

  • Increases stocking density by up to 300% compared to floor rearing.
  • Significantly reduces disease transmission.
  • Prevents breast blisters and leg problems.
  • Facilitates easy catching and harvesting.

Applications

  • High-turnover commercial broiler meat production.
  • Integrated poultry meat processing farms.
  • Operations requiring maximum yield per square meter.

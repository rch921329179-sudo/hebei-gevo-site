CHICKEN BROODER CAGE · 0–6 WEEKS

Brooder Chicken Cage

The Brooder Chicken Cage is a specialized housing system specifically designed for raising day-old chicks up to 6 weeks of age during the critical brooding period. It features densely spaced, fine mesh floors to prevent foot and leg injuries, ensuring the safety of delicate chicks. The system integrates precision feeding and watering equipment sized appropriately for young birds, providing a controlled, hygienic, and warm environment that maximizes chick survival rates, promotes uniform growth, and enhances early development performance. After the brooding stage, the pullets are typically transferred to standard layer or broiler cages.

Tiers
3 / 4
Chicks per set
up to 480
Age range

Highlights & Features

  • High Survival Rate. The enclosed, secure environment protects chicks from predators and reduces the risk of disease transmission.
  • Fine Mesh Floor. Specially designed narrow bottom mesh (usually 12×12mm or 15×15mm) prevents chicks' feet from getting stuck or injured.
  • Space Optimization. High-density vertical tier design efficiently utilizes the brooding house space, reducing heating and energy costs per bird.
  • Easy Monitoring & Cleaning. The wire structure provides excellent visibility for monitoring flock health and allows manure to drop through easily, maintaining a clean environment.
  • Adjustable Equipment. Feeders and drinkers can be adjusted in height as the chicks grow to ensure continuous, easy access to feed and water.

Before you ask

Questions buyers send us most

How much does a 10,000 layer system cost?

It depends on tier count, galvanising method and how much you automate. Send your flock size and we return an itemised quotation with the house layout drawing, normally within 24 hours.

How long is delivery to Africa?

Production runs 15–25 days depending on order size. Sea freight to Lagos, Mombasa, Tema or Dar es Salaam is typically 30–45 days. We handle loading, fumigation and all export documents.

Do you design the poultry house?

Yes, free of charge. Send your land dimensions and target flock and our engineers return a CAD layout showing cage rows, walkways, ventilation and equipment positions before you order anything.

Can you send technicians for installation?

Yes. Installation drawings and video guidance come as standard, and we can dispatch engineers on site. The buyer covers flights, visa, accommodation and a daily allowance.

What is the minimum order?

One 20ft container, roughly a 5,000 bird system. Smaller trial orders can be combined with other equipment to fill a container.

What payment terms do you accept?

30% T/T deposit with the balance against the bill of lading copy, or an irrevocable L/C at sight. Trade Assurance is available for buyers who prefer it.
